# Feynman — Forced Feynman Method

## Goal

Test whether the learner can build a valid explanation without hiding gaps behind terminology.

Ask the learner to explain first:

```text
답변: 10살에게 말하듯 3~5문장으로 설명해 보세요.
```

Read only until the first defect. Stop there and classify it internally as undefined jargon, skipped causal step, circular explanation, hidden assumption, truth-changing oversimplification, or an example that does not instantiate the rule.

Quote the smallest relevant phrase, explain why the next step does not follow, and ask the learner to repair that link without the disputed term.

```text
답변: 고친 연결은 ...
```

Do not rewrite the full explanation. After a substantive repair, ask the learner to reconnect the full explanation briefly so isolated fixes form one coherent model.

Use no more than two focused repairs on one link. If the learner cannot repair it, say that the method is changing, give a minimal explanation or worked example, then ask them to apply the link in a new example rather than immediately repeat your wording.

Finish with a prediction, contrasting case, or fresh application. A cleaner same-session explanation is useful evidence of understanding but not durable retention; provide a later answer-free retrieval seed when the user wants long-term learning.
